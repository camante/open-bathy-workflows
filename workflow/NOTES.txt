River skeleton update (no cross-sections)
========================================

Included:
  - bathy_main.py (updated): adds --river-method skeleton|xs (default skeleton)
  - river_domain_mask.py: builds river channel mask using NHD flowlines + waffles water mask
  - river_skeleton_bathy.py: generates river bed elevations via distance transforms (no XS)
  - xs_builder.py + xs_infer_bathy_raster.py unchanged (legacy river-method xs)

Key new CLI args (bathy_main.py):
  --river-method skeleton|xs
  --river-channel-buffer-m <m>
  --river-max-channel-width-m <m>
  --river-mainstem-min-order <int>
  --river-max-mainstem-width-m <m>
  --river-shape-exp <float>
  --river-dmax-min-m <m>
  --river-dmax-max-m <m>
  --river-save-skeleton-debug

How it decides river vs ocean:
  - Takes NHD flowlines from river_network.gpkg as a seed
  - Buffers them by --river-channel-buffer-m
  - Intersects with waffles water mask (water=0)
  - Rejects pixels wider than width-threshold (distance-to-bank proxy)
  - Result is a 'river_channel_mask' that avoids filling bays/ocean

Using extra XYZ soundings for river (skeleton method)
----------------------------------------------------
If you provide soundings via any of:
  - --extra-xyz <file1,file2,...>
  - --extra-xyz-cudem=hydronos,ehydro (downloads + converts to XYZ)
  - --river-soundings <file1,file2,...>   (hidden alias)
then bathy_main will pass those into river_skeleton_bathy.py to:
  1) refine the per-centerline Dmax prior (via implied-Dmax from soundings), and
  2) optionally enforce observed depths at the sounding grid cells (anchors).

Related CLI args (bathy_main.py):
  --river-soundings-mode auto|depth_pos|depth_neg
  --river-soundings-max-dist-m <m>
  --river-soundings-min-r <0-1>
  --no-river-soundings-enforce

Important assumption:
  - Sounding Z values are treated as *depths* (not bed elevations). Use
    --river-soundings-mode to specify whether they are positive-down or
    negative-down depths.
