Phase 89: Expanded channel-surface recovery before bank-derived fallback

What changed
- geo/raster_ops.py
  - compute_depth_from_bed_and_dem() now adds another in-channel recovery step before bank fallback:
    1. same_component_surface
    2. adjacent_channel_surface
    3. expanded_channel_surface
    4. bank_ring_surface
    5. unresolved
  - expanded_channel_surface searches a limited wider channel neighborhood before using bank-derived WSE
  - receipts now report expanded-channel support counts and component ids

Why
- Some components still used bank-derived fallback only because their nearest usable in-channel support was a few channel pixels farther away than the immediate adjacency halo.
- This keeps recovery in-channel longer before using bank-derived support.
