Phase 88: adjacent-channel depth-surface recovery before bank fallback

Summary
- Reduced another avoidable bank-derived WSE fallback case in the active structured river route.
- Components with DEM gaps but no in-component DEM support now try adjacent in-channel component surface support before falling back to bank-ring-derived WSE.

What changed
- geo/raster_ops.py
  - compute_depth_from_bed_and_dem() now adds an intermediate recovery mode:
    - adjacent_channel_surface
  - New receipt fields:
    - components_using_adjacent_channel_surface_propagation
    - per-component adjacent_channel_valid_pixels
    - per-component adjacent_component_ids
    - used_adjacent_channel_surface_propagation
  - Recovery order is now:
    1. same_component_surface
    2. adjacent_channel_surface
    3. bank_ring_surface
    4. unresolved

Tests
- Updated per-component recovery-mode test to reflect adjacent-channel recovery.
- Added regression proving adjacent channel support is used before bank fallback when a no-support component is close to another valid channel component.
